About the Expo

The Uganda International Coffee Expo 2026 brings together farmers, processors, exporters, researchers, buyers and other stakeholders to share knowledge across the coffee value chain.

The event provides a platform for exhibitions, discussions, networking and showcasing innovations. Participants can learn about quality, market access, technology and sustainable production.
